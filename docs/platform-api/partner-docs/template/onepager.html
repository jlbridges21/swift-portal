Same SkyyTask job, a much better handoff

Everything up to “job done” stays exactly as it is in {{partner.name}}. ShootPortal steps in only for the last mile: getting the files from the {{partner.contributor}} to the {{partner.recipient}}, beautifully and trackably.

A

The workflow, step by step

1

A job is set up in {{partner.name}}, like today

{{partner.name}} quietly tells ShootPortal the job's address, the {{partner.recipient}} and the {{partner.contributor}}. Nobody has to type anything twice.

2

The {{partner.contributor}} taps “{{partner.button}}”

A {{partner.name}}-branded upload screen opens right inside the app.

3

The {{partner.contributor}} uploads and taps “Submit delivery”

Photos, video, documents or inspection files, from phone or computer. If the signal drops, it picks up where it left off.

4

The {{partner.recipient}} gets their private delivery page

By a branded email from us, a link in your {{partner.name}} chat, or both, whichever you prefer.

5

The {{partner.recipient}} views and downloads

Everything organized by type, available for {{pricing.retentionDays}} days.

6

{{partner.name}} is told what happened, automatically

“Delivered,” “client viewed,” “client downloaded,” so you can close the job or release the {{partner.contributor}}'s pay without checking by hand.

What {{partner.name}} keeps

  • The job, bidding and pricing
  • The client relationship and your chat
  • Payments and pilot payouts
  • Your brand, front and center

What ShootPortal handles

  • The branded upload screen for pilots
  • Safe storage for every file type
  • The branded delivery page and emails for clients
  • Automatic updates back to {{partner.name}}
