A delivery's size is the total of all files the pilot uploads for one job (1 GB = 1,000,000,000 bytes). Links such as 360° tours count as zero. The price is fixed the moment the delivery is submitted and appears in the API and status updates immediately.

$199 monthly minimum

Each month is the total of that month's deliveries. If it's under $199, the invoice is $199. Once deliveries pass $199, you pay only the total, never total + minimum.

Launch credit

The first 50 GB of deliveries are on us, and the monthly minimum is waived during the launch period. Statements still show each delivery's normal price, marked “covered by launch credit,” so you can see what it would cost.

90 days included · extended storage

Every delivery stays available for 90 days. Need an archive (e.g. enterprise inspections)? Keep a delivery longer for $0.05 per GB per month, opted in per delivery.
