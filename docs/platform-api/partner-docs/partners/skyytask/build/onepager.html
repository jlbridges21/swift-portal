The idea in one sentence

SkyyTask keeps the job, the chat and the client. ShootPortal quietly takes over the files: a branded upload for the pilot, a beautiful delivery page for the client, and an automatic update back to SkyyTask at every step.

For pilots

One button in the SkyyTask app. Upload photos and video right there, even big files on a spotty connection. No more Drive folders.

For clients

A private, SkyyTask-branded page to view and download everything. No codes, no accounts, no Google Drive.

For SkyyTask

Automatic updates when the pilot delivers and when the client opens and downloads. You stay in control of everything else.

01 · How it would work

Same SkyyTask job, a much better handoff

Everything up to “job done” stays exactly as it is in SkyyTask. ShootPortal steps in only for the last mile: getting the files from the pilot to the client, beautifully and trackably.

Today
Complete JobOTP codeGoogle Drive link
  • Clients juggle a code and leave the app for Drive
  • Unbranded folders; no view or download tracking
  • Pilots set up folders and sharing by hand
With ShootPortal
Transfer deliverablesUploadBranded client page
  • The upload screen opens inside the SkyyTask app, already tied to the job
  • The client gets one private link: no code, no login
  • SkyyTask hears back automatically at every step
A

The workflow, step by step

1

A job is set up in SkyyTask, like today

SkyyTask quietly tells ShootPortal the job's address, the client and the pilot. Nobody has to type anything twice.

2

The pilot taps “Transfer deliverables”

A SkyyTask-branded upload screen opens right inside the app.

3

The pilot uploads and taps “Submit delivery”

Photos, video, documents or inspection files, from phone or computer. If the signal drops, it picks up where it left off.

4

The client gets their private delivery page

By a branded email from us, a link in your SkyyTask chat, or both, whichever you prefer.

5

The client views and downloads

Everything organized by type, available for 90 days.

6

SkyyTask is told what happened, automatically

“Delivered,” “client viewed,” “client downloaded,” so you can close the job or release the pilot's pay without checking by hand.

What SkyyTask keeps

  • The job, bidding and pricing
  • The client relationship and your chat
  • Payments and pilot payouts
  • Your brand, front and center

What ShootPortal handles

  • The branded upload screen for pilots
  • Safe storage for every file type
  • The branded delivery page and emails for clients
  • Automatic updates back to SkyyTask

03 · Pricing, what's next & what you'll get from me

A simple price per delivery, and a clear path to launch

A

The pricing I'm proposing

Initial pricing — subject to change

One flat price per delivery, based only on how big it is. You'd know what every job costs the moment it's delivered, and can work it out before the job even starts.

Above 100 GB
Large enterprise mapping, point clouds

$199/month minimum

Only applies if a month's deliveries add up to less. Past $199, you pay just the total.

Launch credit

The first 50 GB of deliveries are free, and the minimum is waived while we launch.

90 days included

Every delivery stays up 90 days. Need longer? $0.05 per GB per month.
